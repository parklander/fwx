Daily grids from the Canadian Wildland Fire Information System (NRCan), noon local standard time. Alerts, RDPS wind and precipitation and GOES satellite images from Environment and Climate Change Canada. Satellite fire detections from NASA FIRMS (VIIRS and MODIS); reported fires from CWFIS. The PDF covers the day before the selected date through the end of the CWFIS forecast.
